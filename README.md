# Hi, I'm Nuhamin

**AI is only as safe as the people who build it and use it.**

I help organizations adopt AI safely and get real value from it, combining **AI governance**, **cybersecurity** and **people development**. I'm a published AI researcher completing an M.S. in Cybersecurity (December 2026), with six years of experience in risk and program management.

🌐 **Portfolio:** [nuhamin-bit.github.io](https://nuhamin-bit.github.io)

## My AGE approach

- **Assess:** find where AI can go wrong, from security and privacy to bias and accuracy, before it hurts anyone
- **Govern:** use frameworks like NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act to turn those risks into practical controls
- **Enable:** help people build real confidence with AI, and measure whether it's working

## What I work on

- **AI governance and risk:** turning AI frameworks and regulation into controls people actually follow
- **Human risk and AI readiness:** measuring how ready people are for cyber and AI risk, and whether training changes behavior
- **Agentic AI security:** what breaks when AI agents are given tools to act, from prompt injection to software supply chain risk
- **AI fluency and enablement:** helping people describe tasks clearly, decide what to delegate, judge AI output and use AI responsibly
- **Security and compliance:** vulnerability assessments mapped to NIST, HIPAA, GDPR and PCI DSS
